# Capturing and organizing energy-backup inquiries

[Español](README-es.md) · [Inicio / Home](../README.md)

**Tools:** Google Forms · Google Sheets · Airtable · AI assistance.
**Context:** a process used with real inquiries at Aponwaonet.

## Challenge
Collect requirements from customers seeking electrical backup and organize the information for follow-up.

## My contribution
Built a Google Forms questionnaire connected to Sheets for automatic response capture. Cleaned records and manually imported them into Airtable. Used AI to help transform price lists before importing them into the product catalog.

## Deliverable and use
Organized customer and product records and an Airtable dashboard covering estimated monthly inquiries, response times and the highest customer-stated budget among requests. This budget represents a stated requirement, not a completed sale.

The process handled a small number of real inquiries. Requirements analysis and equipment sizing remained manual with AI assistance; transfer from Sheets to Airtable was a manual import.

## Evidence and scope
Private screenshots of the form, responses and catalog are retained. Customer data is not published. No time reduction or sales increase attributable to this process was measured.

The [structure example](../01-leads-automation-example.json) uses fictional data to explain fields; it is not an execution result.

# Scholarship and student-benefit reporting

[Español](README-es.md) · [Inicio / Home](../README.md)

**Tools:** Google Sheets · Looker Studio.
**Context:** Universidad Metropolitana, January–November 2023.
**Role:** Program Control and Monitoring Coordinator.

## Challenge
Monitor scholarships and benefits as records changed throughout the quarter. SAP reports were requested from the responsible department by email or in person; assistantship reporting included checkpoints before and after enrollment and in week 8.

## My contribution
Cleaned and organized records in Google Sheets and built a Looker Studio dashboard for quarterly reports covering beneficiaries, applications, approvals and the share of beneficiaries relative to enrollment.

## Deliverable and use
Reports and a dashboard reviewed by the Secretary General, Finance, IT and the Dean of Students. My contribution organized information for institutional monitoring.

## Retained evidence
A working matrix for T2223-2 and dashboard review comments for T2223-3 are retained. They concern different quarters and are not a complete copy of the final deliverable.

The matrix summary records 779 student beneficiaries and 798 benefits. These are documentary totals from that working version, pending reconciliation with individual records, rather than an independently verified count.

## Scope
The process combined manual requests and updates with Looker Studio visualization. No unmeasured time savings or percentage improvements are claimed. A later SQL reconstruction will be distinguished from the work carried out in 2023.

Institutional documentation remains private. The [illustrative CSV](../04-scholarships-sample.csv) contains entirely fictional records to distinguish people from benefits; it does not reproduce UNIMET data.

## Overview

Comprehensive financial feasibility analysis and cost model for a solar + battery energy infrastructure project in the hospitality sector, determining investment viability, payback period, and regulatory compliance requirements.

### The Challenge
- High diesel dependency: 50+ liters/day @ $2-3/liter
- Annual fuel + maintenance: ~$50,000 USD
- No backup during grid outages
- Uncertain regulatory environment for autonomous systems
- Need for data-driven decision on $250,000+ capital investment

### The Solution
- 12-month energy consumption baseline analysis
- System design with 25 kWp solar + 50 kWh battery (economic option)
- NPV calculation @ 10% discount rate (10-year horizon)
- Sensitivity analysis (equipment cost, diesel price, degradation)
- Regulatory compliance verification

## Financial Analysis

**Baseline (Current State - 10 years):**
```
Annual Diesel Cost: $45,000
├─ Fuel: 50 L/day × $2.50 × 365 = $45,625
└─ Maintenance: $5,000
Total Annual: $50,625
Total 10-year: $506,250
```

**Proposed System (Economic Option):**
```
CAPEX: $385,000
├─ Equipment: $225,000
├─ Installation/Engineering: $70,000
├─ Administrative: $33,000
└─ Contingency (10%): $57,000

Annual O&M: $3,000 (inverter maintenance, battery monitoring)
Total 10-year cost: $415,000

Net Benefit (10 years): $506,250 - $415,000 = $91,250
NPV @ 10%: $165,000 USD
Payback Period: 2.58 years
```

---

## System Design

**Economic Option (Recommended):**
- Solar: 25 kWp (50 panels × 500W)
- Battery: 50 kWh (LiFePO4 Lithium)
- Inverter: 40 kW hybrid
- Autonomy: 9 hours continuous operation
- Delivery: 8 weeks
- Margin: 22%

**Optimal Option (Premium):**
- Solar: 35 kWp (25% larger for seasonal variation)
- Battery: 75 kWh (premium LiFePO4)
- Inverter: 50 kW advanced
- Autonomy: 12+ hours continuous operation
- Delivery: 10 weeks
- Margin: 28%

## Sensitivity Analysis

**Equipment Cost Variation:**
- -10%: Payback = 2.33 years (even better)
- +10%: Payback = 2.85 years (still viable)
- +20%: Payback = 3.14 years (acceptable)

**Diesel Price Variation:**
- If diesel rises to $3/liter: Payback = 2.12 years (faster)
- If diesel drops to $2/liter: Payback = 3.08 years (still solid)

**Battery Degradation:**
- 0.5% annual degradation: Minimal impact on 10-year ROI
- Replacement in year 8: Factored into model

# Exploratory energy analysis and proposal for hospitality

[Español](README-es.md) · [Inicio / Home](../README.md)

**Focus:** document review · AI-assisted research · executive presentation.
**Deliverable:** a commercial presentation with alternatives and preliminary estimates for technical and financial assessment.

## Challenge
Explore electrical-backup and photovoltaic-generation alternatives in relation to a hotel's operational continuity needs.

## My contribution
Developed an executive presentation using electricity bills and research. Used iterative AI consultations to explore scenarios and structure the proposal, connecting billing information with operational needs and cost considerations.

## Work product
A presentation prepared to communicate energy alternatives and their potential commercial rationale. Supporting material includes bills, research documents and proposal versions.

## Method and scope
Investment and payback estimates were developed in documents through AI-assisted iterations. They are preliminary scenarios requiring technical and financial verification, not realized savings or a validated Excel financial model.

The proposal was prepared but was not presented to its intended recipient. No completed installation, demonstrated return or completed regulatory verification is claimed.

## Public presentation
The method is described without identifying the establishment or publishing bills, contracts or private commercial documents. Original material is retained privately and is not included in this repository.
